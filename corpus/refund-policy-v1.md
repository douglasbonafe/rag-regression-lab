# Refund Policy (2024)

Customers may request a full refund within 14 days of purchase. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 10 business days. Annual plans are not eligible for partial refunds after the refund window closes. Refund requests must be emailed to billing@nimbus.example with the invoice number.
