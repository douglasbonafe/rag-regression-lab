# Refund Policy (2025)

Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of purchase. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days. Annual plans cancelled after the refund window receive a prorated credit for unused months. Refund requests are submitted from the Billing page in account settings. Refunds are not available for add-on usage charges such as extra AI credits.
