# Account Security

Password reset links expire after 1 hour. Two-factor authentication can be enabled with an authenticator app or a hardware security key. Workspace owners can enforce two-factor authentication for all members. After 5 failed login attempts the account is locked for 15 minutes. Single sign-on with SAML is available on the Enterprise plan.
