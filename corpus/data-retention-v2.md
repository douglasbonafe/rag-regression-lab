# Data Retention (2025)

Deleted workspaces are kept for 30 days before permanent deletion. During this period a workspace owner can restore the workspace from the Trash page. Encrypted backups are retained for 90 days. Customers on the Enterprise plan can request a custom retention period of up to 7 years. Export files are available for download for 7 days.
