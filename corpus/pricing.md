# Pricing

The Starter plan costs $8 per user per month and includes 5 GB of storage per user. The Pro plan costs $15 per user per month and includes 100 GB of storage per user. The Enterprise plan is priced by contract and includes unlimited storage. Paying annually saves two months compared with monthly billing. Prices exclude local sales tax.
