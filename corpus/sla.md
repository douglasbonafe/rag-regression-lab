# Service Level Agreement

Nimbus guarantees 99.9% monthly uptime for Pro and Enterprise plans. If uptime falls below 99.9%, customers receive a service credit of 10% of the monthly fee. If uptime falls below 99.0%, the service credit increases to 25% of the monthly fee. Service credits must be claimed within 30 days of the incident. Scheduled maintenance is announced 72 hours in advance and does not count as downtime. The Starter plan has no uptime guarantee.
