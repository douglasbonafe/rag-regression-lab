# Incident Escalation (internal)

A severity 1 incident is a full outage or a confirmed data breach. Agents must page the on-call engineer within 15 minutes of detecting a severity 1 incident. Severity 2 incidents are escalated through the engineering queue within 4 hours. Customer communication for severity 1 incidents is owned by the incident commander.
